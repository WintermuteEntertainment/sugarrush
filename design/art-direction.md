# Art Direction

## Style

**Animated, not cartoony.** Think South Park's cut-out aesthetic, but refined and polished by actual animators. Flat 2D, bold outlines, simple geometric shapes — expressive through simplicity rather than detail.

Key traits:
- **Flat 2D construction-paper feel** — but clean, not rough
- **Bold outlines** on characters and objects
- **Simple shapes** — characters built from basic geometric pieces
- **Limited but deliberate color palette** — warm, inviting, not clinical
- **Expressive faces** with minimal features (dots for eyes, simple mouths) that convey a lot
- **Smooth, intentional animation** — not the jerky "cheap" look of early South Park, but the deliberate simplicity of later seasons

## Characters

- Paper-doll construction: body parts are separate pieces (allows easy customization of hat/face/shirt/shoes)
- Front-facing default, with side views for movement scenes
- NPCs follow the same style — visually consistent world
- The robot companion: slightly different aesthetic (more rounded, glowing screen-face) to stand apart as "tech"

## Environments

- Flat backgrounds with subtle depth (foreground/midground/background layers)
- Locations: bedroom, kitchen, school hallway, cafeteria, park, gym, restaurant, doctor's office, friend's house
- Each location has a distinct color palette so players immediately know where they are
- Day/night lighting shifts — warmer tones in morning, cooler in evening, dark blue at night

## UI / HUD

- Clean, modern, high-contrast
- HUD elements styled to match the game world (not clinical/medical looking)
- BG meter uses the game's color language (green/yellow/red) with patterns for colorblind accessibility
- Fonts: rounded, friendly, highly readable at small sizes. Two weights: regular for body, bold for numbers/emphasis.

## Mini-Games

- Same art style as narrative scenes — consistent world
- Food illustrations: simple but recognizable, slight exaggeration for clarity (a big bright apple, a clearly-labeled juice box)
- Interactive elements have subtle glow/pulse to indicate they're tappable/draggable

## Screen Effects

- Low BG: screen edges darken, slight wobble/tremor on all elements, desaturation
- High BG: gaussian blur/haze, slight sepia shift, elements respond sluggishly
- These layer on top of the normal art — the style itself doesn't change, just the post-processing

## Tone

- Warm, not sterile
- Inclusive — diverse skin tones, body types, family structures in the art
- The world looks like a place you'd want to be in, not a medical pamphlet
